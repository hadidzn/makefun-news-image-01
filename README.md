# World News — Friday 13 November 2026

[![make.fun · news-image-01](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-01)

A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026
in one image: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns, Ukraine peace talks bring
Europe to the table, Iran says it struck a US radar site in Bahrain, and a
US communications failure halts flights nationwide.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024 JPEG) to
`/output/entry.png`, deterministically, standard library only.
